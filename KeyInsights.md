# Key Insights

* Blinkit generated approximately **₹1.20M in total sales** across **10 outlets and 16 product categories**.
* **Fruits & Vegetables** recorded the highest overall sales, followed by **Snack Foods**.
* **Supermarkets** contributed the largest share of outlet revenue, accounting for nearly **66% of total sales**.
* **Tier 3 cities** generated the highest sales at approximately **₹472K**, followed by Tier 2 and Tier 1 cities.
* **Medium-sized outlets** contributed the largest share of sales at approximately **42%**.
* **2018** was the strongest outlet establishment year based on sales performance.
* **OUT035** was the highest revenue-generating outlet in the analysis.
* **Household products** recorded the highest average sales among the analyzed categories.
* **Meat** had the highest average product rating at approximately **3.98**.
* **Seafood** was the lowest-performing category by overall sales.
* Item visibility remained relatively consistent across categories, suggesting that visibility alone did not explain sales differences.
* The analysis highlights stronger performance from **Supermarkets, Tier 3 locations, medium/high-sized outlets, and high-demand categories**.

### Overall Takeaway

Blinkit's sales performance is concentrated around **Supermarkets, Tier 3 locations, medium-sized outlets, and high-demand categories such as Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods**, while lower-performing categories such as Seafood and Baking Goods provide opportunities for targeted improvement.
